INVESTIGATOR TASK
-----------------
1. Identify the DNS record active during the incident window.
2. Correlate its hostname and IP address with the TLS certificate.
3. Confirm the matching registry record and its status.
4. Record the registry record ID and certificate serial number.

FLAG CONSTRUCTION
-----------------
Use the following format:

FLAG{record_id_certificate_serial}

For the record ID, use the exact identifier shown in the asset
registry. For the certificate serial, use the exact serial shown
in the TLS certificate analysis. Convert letters to lowercase
and replace hyphens with underscores in the final flag.

Do not select an asset based on its hostname alone.
All required values must be supported by the evidence.
