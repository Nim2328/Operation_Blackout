BLACKOUT INCIDENT - FINAL INVESTIGATION BRIEF
=============================================

Case Reference:
BLACKOUT-RE-08

The recovered executable is believed to contain the final
validation mechanism used by the BLACKOUT threat actor.

Your task is to perform reverse engineering on the supplied
ELF executable and determine:

- what input the program expects,
- how the input is transformed or checked,
- which validation condition must be satisfied.

Use static and dynamic analysis to correlate the program
behaviour with the BLACKOUT investigation.

The final investigation flag must be recovered from your
analysis of the executable.

The flag is not provided directly in this brief.
