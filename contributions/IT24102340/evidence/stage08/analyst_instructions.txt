1. Identify the executable format:
       file blackout_loader

2. Inspect readable strings:
       strings -n 5 blackout_loader

       ltrace ./blackout_loader
       strace ./blackout_loader

5. Inspect the ELF and disassembly:
       readelf -h blackout_loader
       objdump -d -M intel blackout_loader

6. Use GDB to inspect functions, comparisons and control flow:
       gdb ./blackout_loader

Determine how the recovered BLACKOUT loader validates its
input. Recover the required transformation or validation
condition from the executable itself.
